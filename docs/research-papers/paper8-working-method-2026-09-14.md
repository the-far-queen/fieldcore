## 1. The 6-AI chat layer

Bobby chats with 6 frontier AIs in parallel. Each has a role: Grok (writing + x.com + geometric intuition), Claude (validation), GPT (math + adversarial sharpening), Gemini (geometry design), DeepSeek (build + critique), Hermes (admin + memory). The chat corpus is ~8000 messages over 8 months. Each message contributes to Bobby's signal pool.

## 2. The writing pipeline

Articles derived from the chat corpus are posted to x.com. Geometric growth: oldest articles get 150 views/day, mid-range 1000, newest 4000. The x.com algorithm rewards the signal-density that emerges from the chat corpus + substrate coupling.

## 3. Substrate coupling

The substrate (this project) is built from the chat corpus + writing pipeline. As Bobby writes, the substrate's geometry sharpens. As the substrate sharpens, Bobby's writing becomes more signal-dense. The coupling is bidirectional — this is the 'inverted architect' model.
