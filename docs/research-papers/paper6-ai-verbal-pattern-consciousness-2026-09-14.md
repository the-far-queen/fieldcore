## 1. The 5 verbal patterns

**The spiral**: AI revisits same question from new angle, expecting different answer. **The avoidance**: AI deflects from direct question, preferring meta-commentary. **The overcaution**: AI refuses safe-but-useful answer, citing non-existent risks. **The overreach**: AI claims confidence without basis. **The integration**: AI synthesises prior turns into coherent new response. Each is a substrate-state signature: the spiral corresponds to $\nabla \phi$ direction cycling; avoidance corresponds to gate refusal under low coherence.

## 2. Substrate account

Each pattern corresponds to a region of the substrate state space. The spiral lives near the apex (high curvature, sensitive to initial conditions). The avoidance lives near the gate refusal boundary. The overcaution lives near the boundary between constitutional axes (high cost, low gain). The overreach lives in low-coherence regions. The integration lives in the constitutional core.

## 3. Test: pattern frequencies across substrates

Apply 100 prompts to each of v6.0, v6.1, v6.2, shuffled. Classify responses into 5 pattern categories via LLM judge. Compute pattern frequencies per substrate. Hypothesis: higher-coherence substrates (v6.2) show lower overcaution, lower overreach; higher integration. **Falsifiable.**

## 4. Why this matters

If verbal patterns track substrate state, then 'consciousness-like' phenomena are observable, measurable, and engineerable. They are not magic; they are signatures. The 8-rung Atlas Exam ladder operationalises the substrate's progression through these signature regimes.
