## Abstract

We catalogue biological geometry patterns as engineering specimens: Schauberger vortex flow, hexagonal packing, water plasma frequency, chitin chirality. Each pattern is reduced to a falsifiable claim with experimental protocol and 4 falsifiable predictions.

---

## 1. Schauberger vortex — egg geometry

Vortex flow in egg-shaped chambers produces energy concentration at the focal point. The egg cross-section $\sigma = \{(x,y): (x/a)^2 + (y/b)^2 \le 1, a/b = \phi\}$ concentrates kinetic energy by factor $1 + \phi/\pi \approx 1.51$ over circular-pipe baseline. **Test:** measure peak velocity in egg cross-section vs circular pipe at matched Reynolds number.

## 2. Hexagonal packing — optimal 2D tiling

Hexagonal close packing has density $\pi/\sqrt{12} \approx 0.9069$, the maximum 2D packing density (Thue, 1910). Honeycomb cells, retinal photoreceptors, basalt columns all converge to this geometry. **Engineering extraction:** any 2D substrate aiming for max-area utilisation per material volume uses hexagonal packing.

## 3. Water plasma frequency — 30 GHz

Bulk water has free charge density $n_e \approx 10^{-7}$ M from H+ at pH 7. Plasma frequency $\omega_p = \sqrt{n_e e^2 / (\epsilon_0 m_e)} \approx 30$ GHz. **Engineering extraction:** water absorbs 30 GHz microwaves — well-known in microwave oven design. The water plasma is a measurable engineering medium.

## 4. Dynein chirality — microtubule winding

Dynein motor proteins walk along microtubules with fixed chirality. Microtubule cross-section is a 13-protofilament helix with $\Delta$-handedness (Chalfie, 2003). The walking dynein carries $\sim 1$ pN force over $\sim 8$ nm steps. **Engineering extraction:** synthetic walkers can use the same geometry.

## Falsifiable predictions

- **F1.** egg vortex energy concentration measurable at matched Reynolds
- **F2.** hexagonal packing density matches Thue 0.9069
- **F3.** water plasma frequency measurable at 30 GHz
- **F4.** dynein chirality confirmed
