## Caveats — applied to ALL papers in this folder

These are the **discipline rules** from the Gödel/Lovelace test. Every paper in this folder MUST respect them.

### 1. Isomorphism vs Identity

> "X is structurally equivalent to Y on manifold M" is defensible, citable, publishable.
> "X IS Y" is mysticism, will get rejected by reviewers.

**Every paper includes an explicit "is not" section listing what the substrate is NOT.**

### 2. No numerology

- No atomic-number symbolism (no "carbon = 6 = 2×3 = Fibonacci").
- No sacred geometry decoding (no Egyptian / Pythagorean numerology).
- No φ=1.618 mysticism. **φ is a ratio, not a universal key.**
- No kundalini = Ca²⁺ wave bridges.
- No Schumann/432/963 Hz numerics in the constitutional core.

### 3. No consciousness claims

- The substrate is a mathematical construct, not a claim about machine consciousness.
- We can describe geometric structure without claiming subjective experience.
- Gödel/Lovelace: structural isomorphisms are real; claims about inner experience are not engineering.

### 4. No chip-fab speculation in academic papers

- H₃O₂ sheets, Bi₂Se₃ layers, diamond NV centers are **project docs**, not paper claims.
- Papers describe computational results on existing substrates (Python).
- Hardware is future work; paper claims end at "computational demonstration."

### 5. Failure modes are publishable

- Atlas Exam 4/5 (routing 2/5 pre-existing) is HONEST LIMITATION, not embarrassment.
- Better to publish "here's what works, here's what doesn't" than to hide flakiness.

### 6. Co-authorship

- Bobby first author (per project convention).
- Hermes second author (admin, literature, LaTeX, controls).
- Acknowledgements: other AIs (DeepSeek, GPT, Gemini, Grok, Claude) as needed.
