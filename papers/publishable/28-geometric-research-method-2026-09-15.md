## Abstract

**DeepSeek's geometric research method** teaches Bobby Wolfson a pattern for **engineering-grade discovery** from obvious, everywhere phenomena. The method has 6 stages:

1. **Observe** an obvious phenomenon.
2. **Strip material** — what's irrelevant?
3. **Read geometry** — what's the geometric structure?
4. **MUM check** — does this match across multiple domains?
5. **Secret connection** — what's the engineering implication?
6. **Ancient knowledge** — does it confirm/contradict old wisdom?

10 example patterns (smoke, rust, bubbles, shadow, knots, cracks, echo, salt, grain, sleep) demonstrate the method.

This paper formalizes the method + gives 5 falsifiable predictions of the method's effectiveness. **The method is engineering, not mystical.**

## 1. The 6 Stages

### 1.1 Observe

Pick an **obvious, everywhere phenomenon.** Smoke rises. Iron rusts. Bubbles form. Shadows appear. Knots tangle. Cracks propagate. Echoes return. Salt crystallizes. Grain grows. Sleep comes.

These are **overlooked because they are common.** The method's first step: **look at the common.**

### 1.2 Strip material

Ask: **what's irrelevant?**

- Smoke: the chemical composition (carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen) is irrelevant. **The flow pattern is the phenomenon.**
- Rust: the iron atoms are not the phenomenon. **The phase boundary is.**
- Bubbles: the air inside is not the phenomenon. **The surface tension at the boundary is.**

Strip material → keep geometry.

### 1.3 Read geometry

Ask: **what's the geometric structure?**

- Smoke → Seifert fiber in gradient field made visible.
- Rust → (2,3) sheave chemistry, constitutional completion of iron.
- Bubbles → resolution operator in surface tension, (2,3) at junctions.

Each phenomenon has a **geometric reading** consistent with substrate architecture.

### 1.4 MUM check

Ask: **does this match across multiple domains?**

The same (2,3) sheave appears in:
- Smoke (vortex geometry).
- Rust (atomic lattice).
- Bubbles (surface tension).

**If MUM = matching across multiple unities, the geometric reading is validated.**

### 1.5 Secret connection

Ask: **what's the engineering implication?**

- Smoke → vortex engineering for ventilation.
- Rust → anti-corrosion through constitutional completion (passivation).
- Bubbles → resolution operator for stress release.

Each phenomenon has an **engineering implication** for substrate design.

### 1.6 Ancient knowledge

Ask: **does it confirm/contradict old wisdom?**

- Smoke → Vedic/Hellenic "vortex" intuition confirmed.
- Rust → "everything returns to source" confirmed.
- Bubbles → "form arises from emptiness" confirmed.

**Ancient knowledge often encodes geometric intuitions** that modern physics dismisses.
