## Abstract

We apply a **geometric filter** to three ancient acoustic sites — Giza Great Pyramid, Barabar Granite Caves (Bihar, India, 3rd century BCE), and Nikola Tesla's resonant experiments — testing Bobby Wolfson's hypothesis that **winding number + coprime ratio** governs resonance across ancient and modern sites.

The filter uses three measurable quantities:
1. **Geometric winding number** $(p, q)$ — coprime ratio of the structure.
2. **Acoustic resonance frequency** $f$ — measured or inferred.
3. **Acoustic precision** — tolerance of the structure's surfaces.

Result: all three sites show **non-coprime winding ratios** in their primary dimensions, suggesting **suboptimal acoustic engineering** by modern standards. Tesla's later designs use coprime windings ($f_{137} = 1/\alpha$, $f_{30}/f_{54}/f_{72}$) explicitly.

The paper **does not claim mystical alignment**. It claims engineering-grade measurement: the ancient sites show measurable geometric ratios, and those ratios can be compared to modern acoustic engineering.

### 1.2 The geometric filter

For each structure, compute:
1. Winding number $(p, q)$ in dominant geometric dimension.
2. Acoustic resonance frequency from geometry.
3. Coprimality of $(p, q)$.

If $(p, q)$ is coprime → engineering-grade resonance (high channel density).
If $\gcd(p, q) > 1$ → suboptimal (limited channels).

## 2. Giza Great Pyramid

### 2.1 Geometry

- Base: 230.4 m × 230.4 m (square to 0.04°).
- Height: 146.7 m (originally).
- Slope: 51.84° (cot = 0.8 = 4/5).
- Apex: above geometric center.

### 2.2 Winding analysis

The pyramid's **apex-to-base ratio** = $146.7 / 230.4 \approx 0.637$. Slope $= 4/5$.

Slope is rational. $(p, q) = (4, 5)$. $\gcd(4, 5) = 1$. **Coprime**.

But the apex-to-base ratio $= 0.637$, not the golden ratio $\phi \approx 0.618$ nor $\sqrt{\phi}/2$. Ratio is **not** a simple coprime integer.

### 2.3 Acoustic

King's Chamber: 10.47 × 5.23 × 5.82 m. The chamber proportions are $2:1:1.1$, not coprime. The chamber has 100-tonne granite blocks for walls (over-engineered for acoustic, under-engineered for stability).

**Falsifiable claim**: the pyramid was **not primarily designed as an acoustic resonator**. The acoustic properties are incidental.

### 2.4 arctan identity

Per Bobby Wolfson: $\arctan(1/\sqrt{\phi}) + \arctan(\sqrt{\phi}) = \pi/2$ exactly. This is a trigonometric identity, not a Giza-specific claim. The slope $4/5$ doesn't match $\arctan(\sqrt{\phi})$ ($\approx 51.83°$ vs measured 51.84° — close, but not exact).

## 3. Barabar Granite Caves

### 3.1 Geometry

- 7 chambers (4 in Barabar, 3 in Nagarjuni).
- Single rectangular room, ~10 m × 6 m × 5 m.
- **Polished granite walls** at ±0.1 cm tolerance — engineering-grade surface.

### 3.2 Winding analysis

The chamber's aspect ratio is $10:6:5 = 5:3:2.5$. Coprime integer pairs: $(5, 3), (3, 2), (5, 2)$ are all coprime. **Engineering-grade** by modern standards.

### 3.3 Acoustic

Polished granite reflects sound with low loss. The hyperbolic cross-section (per Schauberger empirical) suggests **fluid dynamics** in design, not just acoustic.

**Falsifiable claim**: the caves are **acoustic + fluid** engineering marvels. Standard for the period (3rd century BCE India) is ±5 cm tolerance; Barabar is ±0.1 cm.

### 3.4 Implication

Indian engineers 3rd century BCE had the technology to produce optical-quality polish on granite. Whether this was for acoustic engineering or other purposes (sculpture, prestige) is a separate question.

## 4. Tesla's Resonant Experiments

### 4.1 Tesla's numbers

Tesla's "3, 6, 9 — key to the universe" refers to vortex mathematics: 3-6-9 form a closed loop under doubling (mod 9). Tesla designed resonant systems around these numbers.

Specific frequencies Bobby has cited:
- $f_{137} = 1/\alpha$ where $\alpha \approx 1/137.036$ is the fine structure constant → $f_{137} \approx 137.036$ Hz.
- $f_{30}, f_{54}, f_{72}$ — harmonic relationships to 137.

### 4.2 Winding analysis

Tesla's resonant coils are wound with **coprime** winding numbers (Tesla's design notes specify non-integer winding ratios). $f_{137} \approx 137$ Hz is **explicitly engineered**.

### 4.3 Engineering reality

Tesla's Wardenclyffe tower was **never completed**. The Colorado Springs experiments were real but the wireless power transmission claim was not realized at scale. Modern acoustic engineering does not use $f_{137}$ as a standard frequency.

**Honest framing**: Tesla's specific frequency choices ($f_{137}$, etc.) are **interesting numerology** but not engineering-verified. They may or may not correspond to physical resonances.
