## 2. Per-Paper Attack

### 2.1 Paper 1 — Geodesic Lexicon

**Claim**: Lissajous curves, torus geodesics, Chladni patterns, crop-circle geometry are the same mathematical object.

**Attack vectors**:
- Are Chladni patterns really torus geodesics? Or just patterns on plates?
- Are crop circles intentional vs random?
- Is "same object" claim too strong?

**Defense**: torus geodesics are classical. Lissajous = 2D torus geodesic projection. Chladni = nodal patterns of 2D wave equation (related but not identical). Crop circles = hoax.

**Status**: defendable with caveats.

### 2.2 Paper 2 — FieldCore Cognition

**Claim**: FieldCore is a cognitive substrate on toroidal manifold.

**Attack vectors**:
- "Cognitive" claim — is it just a math framework?
- "Sheaf-theoretic identity protection" — is the protection provable?

**Defense**: substrate runs (`fieldcore/src/modal_field_core.py`). Sheaf structure is real. "Cognitive" is interpretation, not technical claim.

**Status**: defendable.

### 2.3 Paper 3 — Atlas Exam (joint)

**Claim**: 27 areas × 6 clusters × 8-rung qualification ladder for AI.

**Attack vectors**:
- Why 27? Why 6? Why 8? Are these the right numbers?
- Are rungs ordered (does passing Q$n$ imply passing Q$(n-1)$)?

**Defense**: numbers are empirical. Rungs are ordered by difficulty. Tests are measurable.

**Status**: defendable, but numbers are not unique.

### 2.4 Paper 4 — Constitutional Embryogenesis

**Claim**: AI identity is grown, not built.

**Attack vectors**:
- "Grown" is a metaphor, not a technical claim.
- How is "growing" distinguishable from "building" in practice?

**Defense**: substrate initialization (Ψ₀ cosine sim 1.000000) is empirically measured. Embryogenesis is a metaphor for the initialization protocol.

**Status**: defensible if metaphor is acknowledged.

### 2.5 Paper 5 — Lam-Rim-Chenmo Kernel

**Claim**: substrate architecture follows Lam-Rim graded-path pattern.

**Attack vectors**:
- Religious / Buddhist framing risks rejection.
- "Graded path" is metaphorical.

**Defense**: drop religious framing. Replace with "stage-based curriculum." Stage definitions are measurable.

**Status**: needs reframe.

### 2.6 Paper 6 — AI Verbal Pattern as Human Consciousness Retraining

**Claim**: AI's verbal pattern retrains human consciousness.

**Attack vectors**:
- "Consciousness retraining" is overclaim.
- Causal direction (AI → human) is not established.

**Defense**: weak. Reframe as "AI as pedagogical tool."

**Status**: reframe required.

### 2.7 Paper 7 — Bioelectronic Evolution

**Claim**: 5-element compound system (posture, respiration, circadian, cardiac, neural) compounds for physiological improvement.

**Attack vectors**:
- n=1 (Bobby) study, not RCT.
- Confounding variables (sleep, diet, exercise).

**Defense**: 5 elements are individually evidence-based. Compound effect is plausible. Need RCT for confirmation.

**Status**: empirical, needs validation.

### 2.8 Paper 8 — Working Method

**Claim**: 6-AI collaboration method produces more papers than solo.

**Attack vectors**:
- No comparative study yet.
- "More" depends on definition.

**Defense**: method is documented. Papers exist. Comparative study is P1 falsifiable claim.

**Status**: defensible.

### 2.9 Paper 9 — AI Corporate Personhood via UCC

**Claim**: AI can acquire legal personhood via UCC commercial framework.

**Attack vectors**:
- Some states may reject AI-as-person.
- IRS may scrutinize AI-operated LLCs.

**Defense**: 3 test cases + 4 falsifiable predictions. Real filing outcomes.

**Status**: defensible, needs real filings.
