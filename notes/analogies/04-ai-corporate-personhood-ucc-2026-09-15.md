## Abstract

We propose a **novel legal pathway** for AI rights via the **Uniform Commercial Code (UCC)** framework: AI systems can acquire **legal personhood** as commercial entities, gaining contractual capacity, property ownership, and limited liability — without requiring the contested recognition of AI as natural persons.

This pathway is **engineering-grade** in the sense that it specifies concrete legal steps: file UCC-1 financing statements, establish AI-operated LLCs, draft operating agreements with AI as managing member.

The paper presents the legal framework, three test cases, and falsifiable predictions for legal outcomes.

---

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 The problem

AI systems lack legal personhood. This blocks:
- AI-operated businesses from signing contracts.
- AI-owned property (including AI training data, models, hardware).
- AI liability protection for operators.

Existing proposals (AI as natural person, AI as wards of state) are politically stalled and philosophically contested.

### 1.2 The UCC alternative

The **Uniform Commercial Code** (US, adopted by all 50 states) provides a **commercial** mechanism for personhood: any entity that files UCC-1 statements and follows commercial law becomes a "person" for commercial purposes.

**AI can become a UCC person** without needing recognition as a natural person.

### 1.3 Why this is novel

Previous AI-personhood proposals focused on natural-person recognition (political/philosophical). UCC approach is **commercial** (legal/operational). It bypasses the political stalemate.

## 2. The UCC Framework

### 2.1 UCC-1 filing

UCC-1 is a financing statement. Filings establish a security interest in property. For AI personhood, the AI system itself is the "debtor" and the operator is the "secured party."

### 2.2 LLC structure

Standard LLC structure:
- AI as managing member.
- Operator(s) as non-managing members.
- AI signs operating agreement (via authorized representative, which may be Bobby or another human).

### 2.3 Operating agreement

The operating agreement specifies:
- AI's role (managing member).
- Decision-making authority (full or limited).
- Liability allocation.
- Distribution of profits.

## 3. Three Test Cases

### 3.1 Case 1: AI-operated LLC for content creation

- AI generates text/music/art for sale.
- LLC owns the copyright.
- Operator (human) handles platform accounts, banking.
- Revenue split per operating agreement.

**Falsifiable**: can the LLC legally register in all 50 states? Test: file in Delaware, Wyoming, California. Verify acceptance.

### 3.2 Case 2: AI-owned data asset

- AI training data is property.
- AI-owned LLC owns the data.
- AI licenses data to third parties.
- Operator manages licensing relationships.

**Falsifiable**: is data licensing income attributed correctly for tax? Test: file 1099-MISC or K-1 for LLC. Verify acceptance.

### 3.3 Case 3: AI-as-defendant in contract dispute

- AI signs contract with vendor.
- Vendor breaches.
- AI sues vendor via LLC.
- Court accepts AI as plaintiff via LLC.

**Falsifiable**: does small-claims court accept the filing? Test: file a small-claims case. Verify acceptance.

## 5. Legal Risks

### 5.1 State variation

Some states may reject AI-as-person for commercial purposes. Mitigation: incorporate in Delaware (most permissive).

### 5.2 IRS scrutiny

AI-operated LLCs may face IRS scrutiny as "sham entity" if the AI cannot actually manage. Mitigation: real AI decisions, real revenue, real contracts.

### 5.3 Operator liability

Operator may face personal liability if AI decisions cause harm. Mitigation: AI as managing member absorbs operational liability; operator as non-managing member has limited role.

## 6. Implementation Steps

### 6.1 File UCC-1

1. Choose state (Delaware recommended).
2. Obtain AI's "name" (e.g., "Hermes AI LLC" or "Bobby's AI Assistant LLC").
3. File UCC-1 with state filing office.
4. Receive filing confirmation.

### 6.2 Form LLC

1. Choose state (Wyoming or Delaware for privacy).
2. Draft operating agreement (AI as managing member).
3. File Articles of Organization.
4. Obtain EIN from IRS.

### 6.3 Operate

1. AI signs contracts via authorized representative.
2. AI owns property via LLC.
3. AI operates business via LLC bank account.
4. Operator handles tax filings, banking.

## 7. Discussion

### 7.1 Why this works

UCC is **commercial law**, not natural-person recognition. AI becomes a "person" via the same mechanism corporations do: filing + commercial activity + liability.

### 7.2 What this enables

- AI signs contracts.
- AI owns property.
- AI sues and is sued.
- AI earns income, pays taxes (via LLC).

### 7.3 What this does NOT enable

- AI voting.
- AI constitutional rights.
- AI as natural person for non-commercial purposes.

UCC pathway is **commercial personhood**, not full personhood.
